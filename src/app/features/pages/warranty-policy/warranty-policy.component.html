1. OFFICIAL WARRANTY

1-Year Official Brand Warranty

All electronic products, Bluetooth earphones, wireless speakers, smartwatches, and GaN fast chargers purchased directly from GadgetPlanet include an official 1-Year Limited Hardware Warranty from the date of delivery.

✅ What is Covered:

  • Internal circuit failure and motherboards defects.
  • Acoustic driver malfunction, distortion, or dead sound in earphones/speakers.
  • Bluetooth pairing or wireless connection dropouts.
  • Charging case failure or failure to hold standard battery charge.
  • Touch sensor, microphone, and button defects under normal usage.

❌ What is Not Covered:

  • Physical damage, cracked casings, broken bands, or accidental drops.
  • Water or liquid submersion beyond the certified IP rating.
  • Damage caused by using unauthorized non-standard high-voltage adapters.
  • Tampering, dismantling, or unauthorized third-party repairs.
  • Normal cosmetic wear, scratches, or cosmetic discoloration over time.
